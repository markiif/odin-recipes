pastitsio

Description

This classic pastitsio layers rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel, and melted mozzarella between sheets of pasta for a comforting, hearty dish. It's baked until golden and bubbling on top, making it perfect for family dinners or meal prepping ahead of time.

Ingredients

  • 12 pastitsio noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 24 oz marinara sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the pastitsio noodles according to package instructions, then drain.
  3. In a large pan, brown the ground beef with the onion and garlic until fully cooked.
  4. Stir in the marinara sauce, basil, oregano, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. In a bowl, mix the ricotta cheese, egg, and half of the parmesan cheese.
  6. In a baking dish, layer noodles, meat sauce, ricotta mixture, and mozzarella. Repeat layers.
  7. Top the final layer with remaining mozzarella and parmesan cheese.
  8. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes.
  9. Remove the foil and bake for another 15 minutes, until the cheese is golden and bubbly.
  10. Let it rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
